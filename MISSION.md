# Mission: Product Thinking (IDG framing)

## Why
Apply product thinking to real public-sector / digital projects at work, so the team builds things that solve the right problem instead of shipping solutions nobody needed. Grounded in the Institute of Digital Government's (IDG) Product Thinking pathway, part of AI 301: Build with AI.

## Success looks like
- When someone at work asks for a solution ("we need a chatbot"), I can turn it back into a problem: who is affected, what they are trying to do, and what is in their way.
- I can use the Five Whys to get from a symptom to a root cause on a live project.
- I can write a 4Cs problem statement (Clarity, Consequence, Cause, Confirmation) that a team agrees on before anything gets built.
- I can define success as an outcome with a metric, not a list of features.
- I can name and test the riskiest assumptions behind a project before committing to build.

## Constraints
- Some prior exposure: knows terms like user needs, MVP and iteration, but has not practised much.
- Lessons are published as shareable claude.ai artifacts as well as local HTML files.

## Out of scope
- General product management craft beyond IDG's pathway (roadmapping, pricing, go-to-market), unless the mission changes.
