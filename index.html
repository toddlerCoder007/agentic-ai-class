Product thinking

Most requests at work arrive as a solution: “we need a chatbot”. These short lessons teach you to find the real problem first, so your team builds the right thing. Based on the Institute of Digital Government’s Product Thinking pathway.

Lessons

  • Lesson 1 · About 12 minutes Solutions in disguise Turn a request back into the problem behind it.
  • Lesson 2 · About 15 minutes Start with the whys Use the Five Whys to get from a symptom to a cause you can fix.
  • Lesson 3 · About 20 minutes The 4Cs problem statement Write one statement your team can agree on before anyone builds anything.

Practise and review

  • Game · Lessons 1 to 3 Problem Quest Travel three lands, help the locals find the real problem, and beat a boss in each.
