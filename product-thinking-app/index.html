Problem Thinker

  1. Turn the request into a problem. Who are our users? What are they trying to do? What’s getting in their way?
  2. Ask why, five times. Go from the symptom to a cause close to the root that you can act on.
  3. Write the 4Cs. Clarity, Consequence, Cause, Confirmation. Four dimensions, one statement, no solutions yet.

Try it at work this week. Next time a request arrives as a solution, ask the three questions before discussing the solution.
